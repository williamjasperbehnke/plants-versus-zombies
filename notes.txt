Plants vs. Zombies — project notes

Current scope
- Backyard world: ten levels on a five-lane lawn, with persistent completion and unlocks.
- Level-specific plant availability: Sunflower and Peashooter, then Wall-nut,
  Potato Mine, Snow Pea, and Cherry Bomb.
- Normal, Conehead, Newspaper, Flag, Buckethead, and Screen Door zombies.
- Flag-led hordes, nearby buffs, overlapping groups, and larger final hordes.
- HUD-free road scouting with exact planned enemy counts.
- Bouncing plant unlocks/trophies and an animated loss sequence.
- Animated plant/zombie Almanac with stats and saved lifetime kill counts.
- Graveyard menus, custom 3x5 menu font, music and three volume controls.
- Separate normal/debug ROMs, backup save records, and legacy-save migration.

Possible next work (not implemented)
- Night and Pool worlds; each world should have ten levels.
- Check performance, mixer levels, and readability on physical GBA hardware.

README.md documents current play/build commands. docs/README.md links the refreshed
screenshots; docs/DEVELOPMENT.md explains runtime boundaries and asset/save formats.
