Plants vs. Zombies — project notes

Implemented
- Ten Backyard levels with one, then three, then five active lanes, with saved unlocks/completion.
- Early dirt beds have clods, pebbles and soft turf edges shared with scouting.
- Level-specific seed selection: Peashooter, then Sunflower, Wall-nut, Potato Mine,
  Snow Pea, Cherry Bomb, Squash, Tall-nut, Repeater and Chomper. Earlier replays retain their roster.
- Skip seed selection when a level offers up to six plants and bring the full roster.
- Larger rosters require exactly six packets; incomplete START shows a red error.
- Squash crouch/leap/crush and Repeater paired projectiles with per-shot recoil.
- Chomper waits open-mouthed, pulls armored ground enemies into its jaws, then
  chews for fifteen seconds with limbs hanging out. No corpse drops from a swallow.
- Chomper has a stem-and-jaw idle loop and four chewing poses; Tall-nut uses a smooth shell.
- Animated six-slot seed tray above a five-by-two plant grid, with grey slot backgrounds
  matching the packet border dimensions and consistent red unaffordable prices.
- Normal, Conehead, Newspaper, Flag, Buckethead, Screen Door, Football and Pole Vaulter zombies.
- Skull jerseys for athletes; vaulters jump the first ordinary plant and drop their pole.
- Tall-nut blocks vaults. Counter plants unlock before their matching new enemy.
- Incoming zombies form a staggered crowd near the yard and keep that layout when the seed menu opens.
- Later levels vary enemy mixes; only the final yard includes every zombie type.
- Silent wave clears; incoming horde cues remain.
- Flag-led hordes, nearby speed/bite buffs, overlapping groups and larger final hordes.
- Road zombies spawn offscreen and walk in; flag cues wait for visible entry.
- HUD-free road scouting with planned road counts; conditional grave spawns are extra.
- Later-level tombstones, damage cracks, crumbling removal and animated zombie emergence.
- Small falling arms and exposed bone stubs, falling protection, grounded corpses
  and fades, plus a flag drop and separate backward death for flag zombies.
- Bouncing seed/trophy rewards with the HUD visible, a raised wooden caption,
  no floating heading or button prompt, and any-button return to level selection.
- Final explosions, mowers and zombie effects finish before the reward begins.
- Football charges and helmets in later hordes; a second zombie Almanac page and
  saved Football and Pole Vaulter kills in current PVZ3 records.
- Animated defeat sequence, record scratch, original scream and retry controls.
- Almanac grids/details, combined durability stats and saved lifetime kill counts.
- Graveyard menus, custom 3x5 font, music and Master/Music/Effects volume controls.
- Separate normal/debug ROMs and SRAM regions, backup records; older save formats are unsupported.
- Current screenshot/animation gallery and one-command build/test/capture refresh.

Known issue deferred
- On a real Game Boy, the user reports a silent freeze after new-seed rewards on
  levels 1-4 and 1-6. The cause is not confirmed and no crash fix has been applied.
- Emulator checks of all reward reveals with populated lawns and complete rendered
  levels 1-4 / 1-6 did not reproduce it. Diagnostic sources are archived in
  build/deferred_reward_investigation for a later investigation.

Possible future work (not implemented)
- Night and Pool worlds, with ten levels per world.
- Performance, mixer and readability checks on physical GBA hardware.
